# Presentation choices without changing the learning workflow

## Selection and coverage

Select interaction (Review/Reviewer), presentation and outputs independently.
Do not repeat supplied goals or interrogate a learner who already requested a full
visual guide. Default to previous behavior when style is unspecified. Preserve
existing approved material; user-requested restyling authorizes layout changes,
not silent scope reduction. Practice documents remain optional and separate.

Maintain one checked concept/source map: definitions, mechanisms, relationships,
steps, distinctions, examples, conditions, exceptions and applications needed for
the agreed scope. Both styles use this map. Mark unavailable image-only evidence
or obscured text; never infer it. A source guide/graph does not establish coverage.

## Visual Learning

For learning the subject, possibly as a main study source. Preserve necessary
detail while changing its arrangement. Use connected prose when it teaches best;
no wall of repetitive callouts or requirement for an illustration on every page.

- Choose the relationship: sequence -> process, differences -> comparison table,
  chronology -> timeline, dependencies -> concept map, components -> labeled
  illustration, reasoning/calculation -> worked steps. Keep definition, visual
  and interpretation near one another, with a coherent concept per visual unit.
- Split crowded pages at conceptual boundaries. More pages are acceptable;
  neither omit essential mechanisms nor make the font smaller to fit.
- Teach common confusions with explicit contrasts and worked situations. A
  mnemonic is an optional added study cue with limits, not a replacement for
  the precise concept. Avoid repeating a takeaway in several decorative boxes.

## Compact Recall

For a last-pass reference, without assuming the learner has mastered it. Prioritize
concise definitions, rules, formulas, thresholds, distinctions, exceptions,
decision cues and traps. Use readable bullet groups, short prose, small tables
and economical diagrams. Remove repetition, decorative spacing and redundant
examples first. Preserve essential units, qualifications, safety exceptions and
working steps. No universal page cap. Negotiate a real scope/size conflict and
label approved abridgment; don't present a reduced cheat sheet as a full course.

## Figures and source updates

Visual Learning authorizes relevant native diagrams as part of its requested
presentation; no separate approval for every figure in Reviewer mode. Review
still requires approval of the proposed write-up. An unrequested major demo or
external service/upload is not authorized by a style choice.

Use inspected source diagrams when accurate, legible and reusable. Otherwise
prefer original native/vector diagrams; an appropriately licensed attributable
online illustration or available image generation may help when the concept
needs it. No required photo search, paid service or generated art per page.
Read the applicable tool/skill before using it. Check visual facts, directions,
units, labels, context and interpretation. Distinguish a conceptual diagram from
measured data; don't invent measurements for a chart. Exclude private overlays
and irrelevant faces. Preserve required credits and local provenance.

Preserve valid instructor approaches. For an explicitly requested older/current
comparison, keep both under useful labels such as **From your materials** and
**Verified clarification / current guidance**. Never present an incorrect claim
as fact. Verify unstable/high-stakes medical, legal and technical guidance against
authoritative primary sources; distinguish jurisdiction, dates, proposed laws
and enacted rules. Publication dates and verification dates are different. Keep
bulky source logs local, while making corrections and their evidence retrievable.

## Device profile and navigation

Select a profile independently of style and interaction; reuse the saved choice.
Infer a clear requested device/orientation; don't repeat setup. These are PDF
layout starting points, not claims about every physical screen:

| Profile | Canvas | Reading approach |
|---|---|---|
| `paper` (default) | 210 x 297 mm, A4 portrait | Existing print layout |
| `computer` | 280 x 175 mm, landscape | Laptop/desktop; centered comfortable reading column, wide-screen canvas |
| `tablet-portrait` | 180 x 240 mm | 11–12 pt body, right pen margin |
| `tablet-landscape` | 240 x 180 mm | Comfortable central column and annotation margin |
| `mobile` | 100 x 178 mm, portrait | 12 pt body, short headings, full-width stacked content |

`tablet` remains an alias for `tablet-portrait` for existing files/commands. Export
separate editions when several devices/orientations are requested; a fixed PDF
doesn't automatically reflow when rotated. Keep the same checked content map.
For mobile, replace wide comparison grids with successive labeled comparisons,
stack paired question/answers and simplify/rearrange diagrams without dropping
labels or conditions. Large practice items continue across pages when necessary.
Landscape pages have less vertical room; verify actual content/continuations.
Do not merely shrink an A4 page onto a phone. Paper or alternate custom layouts
may still be adapted when requested, without hard-coding device manufacturers.
Use one comfortable main column. Side-by-side is for real comparisons or paired
questions, not all reading. Use restrained color, strong contrast and non-color
labels; no tiny footnotes doing the teaching. Add pen space where useful.

Use headings/bookmarks and, for longer guides, a linked topic navigator with
correct destinations. Keep text selectable, captions connected, table headers
repeated and rows/diagrams intact when meaningful. Reflow long comparisons into
full-width rows rather than narrow boxes. Check every final page at useful zoom,
links, bookmarks and small labels via [verification.md](verification.md). Visual
inspection complements scope/accuracy checks; it doesn't replace them.
